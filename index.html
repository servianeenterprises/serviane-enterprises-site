Who we are

A growing Karnataka security and facilities partner.

Serviane Enterprises provides effective, dynamic security and facility services to construction sites, manufacturing units, shopping malls, shops, godowns, stockyards, motor services, housing societies, hospitals, schools and colleges, farmhouses and medium industrial areas.

Our teams combine carefully selected civilian personnel with ex-servicemen for special requirements. Recruitment is methodical, deployment is tailored to client specifications and supervisors help every team remain alert and disciplined.
